#  Expense Tracker

Take control of your finances with a simple and organized expense tracking application. This web app helps you keep track of your income and expenses, giving you a clear picture of where your money is going.

Whether it's your daily spending or monthly budget, everything is stored in one place so you can manage your finances with ease.

##  Features

- 🔐 Secure user authentication
- 👤 Personalized user accounts
- 💸 Add income and expense transactions
- 📊 View your complete financial history
- 📝 Edit or delete transactions anytime
- 🖼️ Upload a profile picture
- 📱 Responsive design for desktop and mobile
- ⚡ Fast, clean, and easy-to-use interface

##  Why Use It?

Managing personal finances shouldn't be complicated. This Expense Tracker provides a simple experience that helps you:

- Keep track of your spending habits
- Monitor your income and expenses
- Stay organized with all your financial records
- Access your data securely from your account
